## Constitutional purpose

This document establishes the mandatory legal, regulatory, contractual and intellectual property governance framework for Veyra and all systems, products, services, technologies and operations developed, owned, controlled, integrated or operated by Veyra.

It establishes the requirements through which Veyra shall:

1. Identify the laws, regulations and legal obligations applicable to its activities.
2. Establish accountability for legal and regulatory compliance.
3. Protect its own intellectual property while respecting the rights of others.
4. Establish lawful ownership, licensing and usage rights over its assets.
5. Govern software, open-source components, AI models, datasets and generated outputs.
6. Protect personal data, confidential information and other legally protected information.
7. Manage contracts, commercial commitments and third-party legal relationships.
8. Address legal obligations throughout the complete product and service lifecycle.
9. Preserve evidence, respond to legal disputes and demonstrate compliance.
10. Prevent technical, commercial or autonomous systems from bypassing legal requirements.

This Constitution does not constitute jurisdiction-specific legal advice. It establishes the foundational governance obligations through which applicable legal requirements shall be identified, interpreted, implemented and verified.

# PART I — Constitutional Foundations and Legal Authority

### Clause 1 — Establishment

Veyra shall maintain a comprehensive legal, regulatory, contractual and intellectual property governance framework applicable to all its organisational activities and systems.

### Clause 2 — Constitutional Authority

This document forms part of the Veyra Constitution and establishes mandatory requirements for all subordinate policies, standards, procedures, architectures, products and services.

### Clause 3 — Scope of Application

This document applies to:

- Veyra as an organisation and its legally responsible entities.
- AION and all other systems developed by Veyra.
- Software, hardware, infrastructure, models, datasets and technical assets.
- Employees, founders, contractors, consultants and authorised contributors.
- Vendors, suppliers, service providers and relevant business partners.
- Products, services, commercial activities and customer relationships.
- Internal and external operations in every applicable jurisdiction.

### Clause 4 — Mandatory Lawful Operation

Veyra shall conduct its activities in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, binding legal orders and enforceable obligations.

### Clause 5 — Legal Compliance Before Operational Convenience

Technical feasibility, commercial opportunity, development speed, financial benefit or operational convenience shall never independently justify unlawful conduct.

### Clause 6 — No Assumed Legal Permission

An activity shall not be treated as legally permissible merely because it is technically possible, commercially common, not explicitly prohibited by internal policy or not yet challenged by an authority.

### Clause 7 — Evidence-Based Compliance

Material claims of legal compliance shall be supported by appropriate, current and verifiable evidence.

### Clause 8 — Continuing Legal Responsibility

Legal compliance shall be maintained throughout the lifecycle of an activity rather than treated as a one-time approval.

### Clause 9 — Proportionality

Legal governance controls shall reflect the nature, scale, complexity, jurisdictional reach and potential consequences of the relevant activity.

### Clause 10 — No Automatic Legal Immunity

Veyra shall not assume that using third-party technology, outsourcing operations, incorporating AI, operating through another entity or delegating a task removes its own legal responsibilities.

### Clause 11 — Distinction Between Law and Internal Policy

Veyra shall distinguish applicable legal obligations from internal constitutional requirements, voluntary standards, contractual commitments and non-binding guidance.

### Clause 12 — Stronger Applicable Protection

Where multiple applicable requirements govern an activity, Veyra shall identify and fulfil the relevant obligations. Where internal constitutional protections are stricter and lawful, those protections shall be maintained.

# PART II — Legal Identity, Corporate Structure and Accountability

### Clause 13 — Legal Identity

Veyra shall establish and maintain an accurate record of the legal identity of every entity responsible for its organisational activities.

### Clause 14 — Legal Formation and Registration

Where formal registration, incorporation, licensing or other legal establishment is required, it shall be completed before undertaking activities that legally depend upon it.

### Clause 15 — Accurate Organisational Representation

Veyra shall not misrepresent its incorporation status, legal personality, ownership, regulatory standing, registration, authorisations or certifications.

### Clause 16 — Entity Structure

Where Veyra operates through multiple legal entities, subsidiaries, affiliates, joint ventures or other organisational structures, their respective legal identities and responsibilities shall be documented.

### Clause 17 — Entity-Specific Obligations

Legal obligations shall be attributed to the correct legal entity. Veyra shall not assume that compliance by one entity automatically satisfies the obligations of another.

### Clause 18 — Beneficial Ownership and Control

Veyra shall fulfil applicable requirements concerning beneficial ownership, corporate control, disclosure and related transparency obligations.

### Clause 19 — Organisational Authority

The authority to enter contracts, incur liabilities, make formal representations and bind Veyra shall be defined and controlled.

### Clause 20 — Delegated Legal Responsibilities

Delegated compliance tasks shall have identifiable owners, defined responsibilities and appropriate oversight.

### Clause 21 — Accountability Preservation

Delegation, outsourcing, subcontracting or automation shall not be treated as automatically transferring away the legal responsibilities of the entity that retains them.

### Clause 22 — Conflicts of Interest

Actual, potential or perceived conflicts affecting legal decisions, intellectual property ownership, compliance assessments or regulatory engagement shall be disclosed and appropriately managed.

### Clause 23 — Corporate Records

Veyra shall maintain corporate and organisational records required by applicable law and necessary to demonstrate its legal identity, authority and governance.

### Clause 24 — Legal Representation

Only appropriately authorised persons may represent Veyra in binding legal, regulatory, contractual or corporate matters.

# PART III — Jurisdictional and Regulatory Applicability

### Clause 25 — Jurisdictional Identification

Veyra shall identify the jurisdictions relevant to its establishment, ownership, operations, personnel, customers, users, infrastructure, data processing and commercial activities.

### Clause 26 — Activity-Based Applicability

Legal applicability shall be assessed against the actual nature and location of each material activity, not solely the location of Veyra's headquarters.

### Clause 27 — Extraterritorial Obligations

Veyra shall assess whether laws or regulations may apply to its activities beyond the jurisdiction in which a responsible entity is established.

### Clause 28 — Market Entry Assessment

Before materially entering a new market or making a product or service available in a new jurisdiction, Veyra shall conduct an appropriate legal and regulatory assessment.

### Clause 29 — No Accessibility-Based Permission

The fact that a product, website, application or AI system can technically be accessed from a jurisdiction shall not be treated as sufficient legal authorisation to actively offer or operate it there.

### Clause 30 — Regulatory Classification

Veyra shall assess whether any product, service, feature, data activity, financial activity, communication service, AI application or other operation falls within a regulated or restricted category.

### Clause 31 — Regulatory Permission

Required registrations, licences, approvals, permits, certifications or other authorisations shall be obtained before the relevant regulated activity begins.

### Clause 32 — Restricted Activities

Veyra shall not undertake activities prohibited by applicable law or activities requiring authorisation that Veyra has not obtained.

### Clause 33 — Regulatory Obligations Register

Veyra shall maintain a structured register of material legal and regulatory obligations.

### Clause 34 — Obligation Mapping

Each material obligation shall, where applicable, be mapped to:

- The relevant jurisdiction and legal authority.
- The applicable law, regulation or binding instrument.
- The affected entity, activity, product or service.
- The specific obligation and its applicability.
- The responsible governance owner.
- Required controls and implementation evidence.
- Relevant deadlines, renewal dates and reporting duties.
- Assessment status and outstanding remediation.

### Clause 35 — Legal Applicability Rationale

Where the applicability of a material legal requirement is uncertain or disputed, Veyra shall record the basis for its interpretation and any necessary professional advice.

### Clause 36 — Regulatory Change Monitoring

Veyra shall maintain a process for identifying material changes to applicable laws, regulations, regulatory decisions and authoritative guidance.

### Clause 37 — Regulatory Impact Assessment

A material regulatory change shall trigger an assessment of its effect on affected entities, systems, operations, contracts, data, products and services.

### Clause 38 — Regulatory Action Tracking

Required actions arising from legal or regulatory changes shall have accountable owners, appropriate priorities, deadlines and documented closure.

### Clause 39 — Regulatory Engagement

Formal communications with regulators, courts and other competent authorities shall be accurate, appropriately authorised and documented.

### Clause 40 — Unresolved Regulatory Uncertainty

Material unresolved legal uncertainty shall be escalated for appropriate assessment. Where necessary, the affected activity shall be restricted or suspended until an acceptable legal position is established.

# PART IV — Legal Risk and Compliance Management

### Clause 41 — Legal Risk Identification

Veyra shall identify legal risks arising from its organisational structure, technology, products, services, operations, contracts, data, personnel and external relationships.

### Clause 42 — Risk Assessment

Material legal risks shall be assessed according to likelihood, potential consequences, affected parties, jurisdictional exposure and available mitigation.

### Clause 43 — Risk Ownership

Each material legal risk shall have an identifiable owner responsible for monitoring, mitigation and escalation.

### Clause 44 — Risk Treatment

Veyra shall establish proportionate controls to avoid, reduce, transfer or otherwise manage identified legal risks.

### Clause 45 — Unacceptable Legal Exposure

Veyra shall not knowingly continue an activity that creates unacceptable legal exposure without appropriate resolution and authorised governance consideration.

### Clause 46 — Compliance Controls

Material legal obligations shall be translated into operational, contractual, procedural or technical controls as appropriate.

### Clause 47 — Compliance Verification

The existence of a policy or control shall not, by itself, be treated as proof of compliance. Its implementation and effectiveness shall be assessed where necessary.

### Clause 48 — Legal Escalation

Material compliance failures, suspected violations, regulatory threats and legal uncertainties shall be escalated through established governance channels.

### Clause 49 — Remediation

Identified legal non-compliance shall trigger proportionate corrective action, including containment, remediation, reassessment and prevention of recurrence.

### Clause 50 — Compliance Exceptions

Internal exceptions may only be considered where lawful, properly authorised, documented and consistent with this Constitution. No internal exception may waive applicable law.

### Clause 51 — Legal Holds

When litigation, investigations, regulatory action or other circumstances create a preservation obligation, Veyra shall establish an appropriate legal hold.

### Clause 52 — Preservation Across Systems

Legal preservation measures shall address relevant repositories, devices, backups, cloud services, connected applications, contractors and third-party systems within Veyra's control or lawful reach.

### Clause 53 — No Improper Destruction

Information subject to a valid preservation requirement shall not be improperly deleted, altered, concealed or destroyed.

# PART V — Intellectual Property Ownership and Rights Governance

### Clause 54 — Recognition of Intellectual Property

Veyra shall respect and appropriately govern intellectual property belonging to itself and other parties.

### Clause 55 — Protected Subject Matter

Veyra shall recognise the possible protection of software, source code, inventions, technical designs, written works, visual works, databases, trademarks, trade secrets, documentation, models and other qualifying intellectual property.

### Clause 56 — Rights Classification

Material intellectual property shall be classified according to its ownership, authorship, licensing, permitted use, confidentiality and applicable restrictions.

### Clause 57 — No Assumption of Ownership

Possession, access, payment, commissioning, modification, hosting or incorporation of an asset shall not automatically be treated as proof of ownership.

### Clause 58 — Ownership and Rights Register

Veyra shall maintain an appropriate register of material intellectual property it owns, licenses, develops, acquires, commissions or otherwise uses.

### Clause 59 — Creator Identification

Where appropriate, Veyra shall identify the original creators, contributors, rights holders and relevant intermediaries associated with material intellectual property.

### Clause 60 — Chain of Title

Veyra shall establish a verifiable chain of title or other sufficient legal entitlement for material intellectual property it claims to own or commercially exploit.

### Clause 61 — Founder Contributions

Intellectual property contributed by founders shall be subject to clear documentation establishing the relevant ownership, assignment, licence or other rights arrangement.

### Clause 62 — Employee Contributions

Ownership and permitted use of intellectual property created by employees shall be governed by applicable law and appropriate employment or separate intellectual property agreements.

### Clause 63 — Contractor and Consultant Contributions

Contracts with contractors, consultants and external contributors shall address ownership, licensing, permitted use, confidentiality and relevant rights in deliverables.

### Clause 64 — Commissioned Works

Commissioning or paying for a work shall not be assumed to transfer ownership unless the applicable law and contractual arrangements establish that result.

### Clause 65 — Assignment Formalities

Where an assignment, licence or other transfer of rights requires a particular form, signature, registration or other legal formality, Veyra shall fulfil that requirement.

### Clause 66 — Moral and Related Rights

Veyra shall respect applicable moral rights, attribution rights and other creator protections, including rights that may not be freely assignable or waivable.

### Clause 67 — Third-Party Rights

Veyra shall not knowingly infringe, misappropriate or unlawfully exploit third-party intellectual property.

### Clause 68 — Proprietary Asset Protection

Veyra shall establish appropriate safeguards for its proprietary software, technical knowledge, inventions, confidential materials and other valuable intellectual property.

### Clause 69 — Trade Secret Protection

Information treated as a trade secret shall receive safeguards appropriate to preserving its secrecy and the legal requirements for trade secret protection.

### Clause 70 — Intellectual Property Registration

Where appropriate, Veyra shall evaluate formal registration, filing, renewal or other protective measures for its intellectual property.

### Clause 71 — Intellectual Property Enforcement

Veyra shall maintain an appropriate process for identifying, assessing and responding to suspected infringement or misappropriation of its intellectual property.

# PART VI — Software, Open-Source and Technology Licensing

### Clause 72 — Software Rights Verification

Software incorporated into or used by Veyra shall have an identified and appropriate legal basis for use.

### Clause 73 — Software Inventory

Veyra shall maintain appropriate inventories of material software components, dependencies, libraries, frameworks and other incorporated technologies.

### Clause 74 — Open-Source Governance

Open-source software shall be used in accordance with the applicable licence and its associated conditions.

### Clause 75 — Licence Classification

Open-source and third-party software licences shall be classified and evaluated according to their relevant obligations and restrictions.

### Clause 76 — Licence Compatibility

Veyra shall assess compatibility where separately licensed components are combined, linked, modified, distributed or incorporated into a larger work.

### Clause 77 — Copyleft Obligations

Veyra shall identify and fulfil applicable copyleft obligations, including requirements that may arise from modification, combination, linking, distribution or other defined forms of use.

### Clause 78 — Source-Disclosure Obligations

Where a licence imposes source-code disclosure or related obligations, Veyra shall determine the circumstances in which those obligations apply and fulfil them where required.

### Clause 79 — Attribution and Notices

Required copyright notices, attribution, licence texts, disclaimers and other notices shall be preserved and provided as required.

### Clause 80 — Commercial Licensing Restrictions

Veyra shall respect the conditions and restrictions governing commercial software, proprietary platforms, APIs, SDKs, development tools and other licensed resources.

### Clause 81 — Licence Compatibility with Business Models

Before incorporating a component into a commercial or proprietary product, Veyra shall assess whether its licence is compatible with the intended development, distribution and business model.

### Clause 82 — Dependency Provenance

Material software dependencies shall have traceable provenance and licensing information where reasonably necessary to establish their legitimacy and permitted use.

### Clause 83 — Unverified Components

Components with unknown provenance, unclear licensing or material unresolved rights issues shall not be incorporated into production systems until appropriately assessed.

### Clause 84 — Licence Changes

Material changes to licensing terms, ownership or availability shall trigger an assessment of their effects on Veyra's products and obligations.

### Clause 85 — Software Distribution

Before distributing software, modified components or derivative works, Veyra shall assess and fulfil the applicable licence and legal obligations.

### Clause 86 — Software Supply-Chain Rights

Veyra shall consider intellectual property and licensing risks throughout the software supply chain, including transitive dependencies and externally maintained components.

# PART VII — Data Rights, Privacy and Information Governance

### Clause 87 — Lawful Data Acquisition

Data shall be acquired, accessed and used through lawful and appropriately authorised means.

### Clause 88 — Data Rights Classification

Veyra shall distinguish among ownership, possession, custody, access, licensing, processing authority, contractual rights and other legal interests in information.

### Clause 89 — Personal Data Compliance

Personal data shall be collected, accessed, processed, stored, transferred, disclosed and deleted in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

### Clause 90 — Lawful Basis

Where a legal basis is required for processing personal data, Veyra shall identify and document the appropriate basis for each relevant processing purpose.

### Clause 91 — Consent Integrity

Where consent is the applicable legal basis or is otherwise required, it shall be obtained, documented, maintained and withdrawn in accordance with the relevant requirements.

### Clause 92 — Purpose and Use Limitations

Data shall not be used beyond applicable legal permissions, contractual rights, disclosed purposes or other binding restrictions.

### Clause 93 — Data Minimisation

Veyra shall apply appropriate data minimisation requirements consistent with applicable law and legitimate system requirements.

### Clause 94 — Data Subject Rights

Veyra shall establish mechanisms for addressing applicable individual rights, including access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and portability where relevant.

### Clause 95 — Sensitive and Protected Information

Special categories of personal data, confidential records, privileged information and other specially protected information shall receive appropriate legal and operational safeguards.

### Clause 96 — Database Rights

Veyra shall assess relevant database rights and restrictions before substantial extraction, reuse, distribution or commercial exploitation.

### Clause 97 — Data Licensing

Datasets and other information assets subject to licensing shall be used only within their permitted scope.

### Clause 98 — Data Provenance

Material datasets shall have appropriate source, collection, authorisation, licensing and transformation records.

### Clause 99 — Unauthorised Data Use

Veyra shall not knowingly use unlawfully acquired, improperly disclosed or unauthorised information.

### Clause 100 — Cross-Border Transfers

International transfers of personal or otherwise protected data shall comply with the requirements applicable to the relevant jurisdictions and data categories.

### Clause 101 — Data Localisation

Where law requires information to be stored, processed or retained in a particular location, Veyra shall implement the relevant restrictions.

### Clause 102 — Data Retention and Disposal

Information shall be retained and disposed of according to applicable legal, contractual, privacy, security and preservation requirements.

### Clause 103 — Third-Party Data Processors

Where third parties process information on Veyra's behalf, appropriate contractual, legal, security and oversight arrangements shall be established.

# PART VIII — Artificial Intelligence, Models and Generated Intellectual Property

### Clause 104 — AI Legal Governance

AI systems developed, integrated, deployed or operated by Veyra shall be subject to relevant legal, regulatory, contractual and intellectual property assessment.

### Clause 105 — AI Model Provenance

Material AI models and model components shall have appropriate provenance records, including their origin, provider, version, licence and material usage restrictions.

### Clause 106 — Model Weight Rights

Model weights, checkpoints, fine-tuned models and other model artefacts shall not be assumed to be unrestricted or freely reusable merely because they are technically accessible.

### Clause 107 — Training Data Rights

Materials used for training, fine-tuning or developing AI systems shall undergo appropriate assessment of provenance, legal basis, licensing and permitted use.

### Clause 108 — Evaluation and Reference Data

Evaluation datasets, benchmark materials, retrieval corpora and reference resources shall be subject to appropriate rights and usage assessments.

### Clause 109 — External AI Services

Third-party AI APIs, hosted models and external AI services shall be evaluated for applicable terms, data handling, intellectual property, confidentiality and other material legal restrictions.

### Clause 110 — Generated Output Ownership

Veyra shall not assume that AI-generated outputs are automatically copyrightable, exclusively owned, assignable or otherwise protected in every relevant jurisdiction.

### Clause 111 — Output Rights Assessment

Where AI-generated outputs are commercially important or legally consequential, Veyra shall evaluate the relevant rights, contractual terms and applicable legal position.

### Clause 112 — Third-Party Rights in Outputs

Appropriate safeguards shall be established to address the risk that generated outputs reproduce, imitate, disclose or otherwise infringe protected third-party material.

### Clause 113 — Human Contribution Records

Where human authorship, creative contribution or other human involvement is relevant to ownership or protection, appropriate records shall be maintained.

### Clause 114 — AI Content Disclosure

Where required by law, contract or applicable product governance requirements, Veyra shall provide appropriate disclosure of AI-generated or AI-assisted content.

### Clause 115 — AI Regulatory Classification

Veyra shall assess the legal and regulatory classification of AI systems according to their actual purpose, capabilities, deployment context and potential impact.

### Clause 116 — High-Impact and Restricted AI Uses

AI applications subject to heightened legal requirements or restrictions shall receive appropriate specialist assessment, safeguards and authorisation before deployment.

### Clause 117 — AI Autonomy and Legal Authority

AI autonomy shall not create authority to undertake legally restricted activities, enter binding commitments or exercise powers that have not been lawfully authorised.

### Clause 118 — AI Legal Accountability

The use of AI shall not be treated as an automatic exemption from the legal responsibilities of Veyra or other legally responsible parties.

### Clause 119 — AI Behaviour Changes

Material changes in model capability, autonomy, data access, external integrations or operating purpose shall trigger an appropriate legal and regulatory reassessment.

### Clause 120 — AION Compliance Boundaries

AION shall operate within the legal, permission, contractual and authority boundaries established by Veyra. It shall not be permitted to circumvent those boundaries through autonomous execution, indirect delegation or external integrations.

# PART IX — Contracts, Commercial Commitments and Third-Party Relationships

### Clause 121 — Contractual Governance

Veyra shall maintain a formal framework for creating, reviewing, approving, executing, managing, amending and terminating material contracts.

### Clause 122 — Authority to Contract

Only appropriately authorised persons or legally valid mechanisms may create binding commitments on behalf of Veyra.

### Clause 123 — Contract Review

Material agreements shall undergo appropriate legal, commercial, operational and technical review before execution.

### Clause 124 — Contractual Clarity

Agreements shall clearly identify the parties, obligations, rights, conditions, limitations, liabilities, applicable law and relevant dispute mechanisms.

### Clause 125 — Customer Agreements

Customer agreements shall accurately describe the products, services, permissions, limitations, responsibilities and commitments involved.

### Clause 126 — Third-Party Agreements

Agreements with vendors, contractors, suppliers, partners and service providers shall establish appropriate responsibilities and legal protections.

### Clause 127 — Intellectual Property Provisions

Contracts shall address ownership, assignment, licences, permitted use, confidentiality and relevant intellectual property rights.

### Clause 128 — Data Processing Agreements

Where required, appropriate agreements shall govern the processing, protection, transfer, access and handling of personal or otherwise protected information.

### Clause 129 — Confidentiality Agreements

Appropriate confidentiality arrangements shall be established where legally and operationally necessary.

### Clause 130 — Third-Party Due Diligence

Material third-party relationships shall undergo risk-based assessment of legal status, rights, licensing, compliance, security and relevant operational dependencies.

### Clause 131 — Subcontracting

Subcontracting arrangements shall be assessed for their effects on legal accountability, confidentiality, data protection, intellectual property and contractual obligations.

### Clause 132 — Supply-Chain Compliance

Veyra shall maintain proportionate oversight of material suppliers and technology providers whose actions may affect its legal obligations.

### Clause 133 — Conflicting Contractual Obligations

Where agreements impose conflicting obligations, Veyra shall identify and resolve the conflict before relying on either obligation as authority for action.

### Clause 134 — Contract Records

Executed agreements, approvals, amendments, renewals and termination records shall be maintained appropriately.

### Clause 135 — Contractual Monitoring

Material contractual obligations, deadlines, renewal conditions and performance requirements shall be tracked.

### Clause 136 — Termination and Survival

Contract termination shall account for continuing confidentiality, intellectual property, payment, data return, deletion, recordkeeping and other surviving obligations.

# PART X — Product, Service and Operational Legal Governance

### Clause 137 — Legal Assessment at Discovery

Material legal and regulatory considerations shall be identified during product and service discovery.

### Clause 138 — Legal Requirements in Design

Applicable legal obligations shall be translated into appropriate product, service, architecture and operational requirements.

### Clause 139 — Legal Requirements in Development

Development activities shall respect applicable licensing, data, confidentiality, employment, contractual and intellectual property obligations.

### Clause 140 — Pre-Release Assessment

Products and services shall undergo appropriate legal and regulatory review before release, proportionate to their nature and risk.

### Clause 141 — Regulatory Readiness

Required registrations, licences, approvals and other permissions shall be obtained before the activities that depend upon them begin.

### Clause 142 — Operational Compliance

Legal compliance shall be maintained during deployment, use, maintenance, monitoring and support.

### Clause 143 — Material Change Assessment

Material changes to intended purpose, autonomy, functionality, user population, data processing, jurisdiction or commercial model shall trigger legal reassessment.

### Clause 144 — Incident-Related Obligations

Legal and regulatory notification, reporting, preservation and cooperation requirements arising from incidents shall be identified and fulfilled within applicable deadlines.

### Clause 145 — Product and Service Suspension

Where continued operation creates a material unresolved legal risk, Veyra shall restrict, suspend or withdraw the affected activity as appropriate.

### Clause 146 — Retirement and Discontinuation

Product and service retirement shall address customer commitments, data, intellectual property, licences, regulatory obligations and required records.

# PART XI — Marketing, Public Representations and Consumer Protection

### Clause 147 — Lawful Communications

Veyra's advertising, marketing, sales and public communications shall comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

### Clause 148 — Accurate Product Claims

Claims about product capabilities, reliability, security, autonomy, accuracy, performance and limitations shall be truthful and supportable.

### Clause 149 — Legal and Regulatory Claims

Veyra shall not claim compliance, certification, registration, approval or authorisation without an adequate factual and legal basis.

### Clause 150 — Fair Commercial Practices

Veyra shall respect applicable consumer protection, competition, advertising, pricing and commercial fairness requirements.

### Clause 151 — Transparent Limitations

Material product limitations, conditions, risks and restrictions shall not be deliberately concealed from customers or users.

### Clause 152 — Comparative Representations

Comparative claims concerning competitors, technologies or products shall be fair, appropriately substantiated and legally permissible.

### Clause 153 — Trademark and Brand Use

Veyra shall respect applicable trademark and branding rights when referencing other organisations, products, services and technologies.

### Clause 154 — Public Legal Statements

Formal public representations concerning legal status, regulatory compliance, intellectual property, disputes or corporate arrangements shall be appropriately authorised.

# PART XII — Disputes, Enforcement and Legal Proceedings

### Clause 155 — Dispute Identification

Veyra shall maintain processes for identifying material legal disputes, infringement claims, regulatory investigations and potential enforcement action.

### Clause 156 — Dispute Escalation

Material legal disputes and threats shall be escalated to the appropriate governance authority promptly.

### Clause 157 — Legal Representation

Legal proceedings and material legal correspondence shall be managed through authorised representatives and qualified legal professionals where necessary.

### Clause 158 — Cooperation with Authorities

Veyra shall comply with valid legal orders, lawful investigations and applicable regulatory requirements.

### Clause 159 — No Obstruction

Veyra shall not knowingly obstruct lawful investigations, destroy relevant evidence, conceal material facts or make knowingly false representations.

### Clause 160 — Privileged Communications

Legal advice and communications subject to legal privilege shall be identified and protected in accordance with applicable law.

### Clause 161 — Privilege Preservation

Access to privileged material shall be restricted appropriately. Veyra shall avoid unnecessary disclosure or actions that may compromise applicable privilege protections.

### Clause 162 — Infringement Claims

Claims alleging infringement, misappropriation or unauthorised use of intellectual property shall be assessed promptly and appropriately.

### Clause 163 — Enforcement of Veyra Rights

Veyra shall establish proportionate processes for enforcing its own intellectual property and contractual rights.

### Clause 164 — Corrective Measures

Where legal non-compliance or infringement is established, Veyra shall take appropriate corrective action.

### Clause 165 — Dispute Records

Material disputes, legal advice, regulatory correspondence, settlements, judgements and remediation measures shall be documented and protected appropriately.

# PART XIII — Legal Records, Audit and Compliance Assurance

### Clause 166 — Compliance Evidence

Veyra shall maintain sufficient evidence to demonstrate the identification, assessment, implementation and fulfilment of material legal obligations.

### Clause 167 — Regulatory Register Maintenance

The regulatory obligations register shall be reviewed and updated when relevant legal, organisational or operational circumstances change.

### Clause 168 — Intellectual Property Register Maintenance

Material intellectual property records shall be maintained with appropriate ownership, licensing, provenance and status information.

### Clause 169 — Licence Register

Material software, model, dataset, content and other licences shall be recorded with relevant restrictions, obligations, renewal conditions and usage rights.

### Clause 170 — Contract Register

Material agreements shall be recorded with their parties, authority, status, obligations, renewal dates and relevant termination conditions.

### Clause 171 — Record Integrity

Legal and compliance records shall be protected against unauthorised access, alteration, deletion, concealment and disclosure.

### Clause 172 — Evidence Traceability

Material compliance decisions shall be traceable to their underlying evidence, responsible decision-makers and relevant review dates.

### Clause 173 — Independent Review

Material legal and regulatory controls shall be subject to appropriate independent or suitably separated review where warranted by risk.

### Clause 174 — Compliance Audits

Veyra shall conduct or commission appropriate audits and assessments of its material legal and regulatory obligations.

### Clause 175 — Audit Findings

Material deficiencies shall be documented, assigned to accountable owners and addressed through corrective action.

### Clause 176 — Retention and Disposal

Legal records shall be retained and disposed of in accordance with applicable legal, contractual, operational and preservation requirements.

### Clause 177 — Compliance Reporting

Material compliance risks, unresolved deficiencies and significant legal developments shall be reported to the appropriate governance authority.

# PART XIV — International Operations and Cross-Border Governance

### Clause 178 — International Operating Readiness

Veyra shall establish the legal and regulatory readiness of a jurisdiction before undertaking material activities there.

### Clause 179 — Local Legal Requirements

Where local laws impose requirements concerning establishment, licensing, consumer rights, employment, taxation, privacy, AI, intellectual property or other matters, Veyra shall address them before undertaking the affected activities.

### Clause 180 — Cross-Border Agreements

International agreements shall be reviewed for applicable governing law, jurisdiction, enforcement, transfer restrictions and relevant mandatory legal requirements.

### Clause 181 — International Data Governance

Cross-border information flows shall be assessed for applicable transfer mechanisms, localisation, confidentiality and data protection requirements.

### Clause 182 — International Intellectual Property Protection

Veyra shall evaluate territorial differences in intellectual property protection, registration, enforcement and contractual rights.

### Clause 183 — Local Regulatory Engagement

Where required or appropriate, Veyra shall engage qualified local professionals or competent authorities to address jurisdiction-specific legal requirements.

### Clause 184 — International Sanctions and Restrictions

Veyra shall assess applicable sanctions, export controls, trade restrictions and other legally binding restrictions relevant to its operations, technology and counterparties.

### Clause 185 — International Tax and Employment Obligations

Where applicable, Veyra shall address tax, employment, payroll, contractor classification and other jurisdiction-specific obligations arising from its international activities.

### Clause 186 — No Automatic Global Compliance

Compliance in one jurisdiction shall not be treated as proof of compliance in another jurisdiction.

# PART XV — Governance, Responsibility and Constitutional Enforcement

### Clause 187 — Governance Ownership

Veyra shall establish clear ownership and oversight of legal, regulatory and intellectual property governance.

### Clause 188 — Professional Legal Expertise

Matters requiring specialist legal, regulatory, intellectual property, tax or other professional expertise shall be referred to appropriately qualified professionals.

### Clause 189 — Independence of Assessment

Material legal assessments shall not be improperly influenced by commercial pressure, development convenience, personal interest or organisational ambition.

### Clause 190 — Compliance Responsibility Across Teams

Legal responsibilities shall be incorporated into relevant engineering, product, security, operations, commercial, data and governance functions.

### Clause 191 — Internal Policy Implementation

Lower-level policies, procedures and standards shall implement this Constitution without weakening its requirements.

### Clause 192 — No Commercial Override

No commercial target, financial commitment, partnership, investment or revenue opportunity may authorise a violation of applicable law or this Constitution.

### Clause 193 — No Technical Override

No software mechanism, autonomous agent, AI model, automated workflow, API, integration or technical workaround may override applicable legal requirements.

### Clause 194 — No Delegation-Based Evasion

Veyra shall not use contractors, subsidiaries, intermediaries, third-party platforms or automated agents to knowingly perform activities that Veyra itself is legally prohibited from performing.

### Clause 195 — Restriction and Withdrawal Authority

Where legal compliance cannot be established or maintained, the relevant product, service, feature, integration or activity shall be restricted, suspended or withdrawn as appropriate.

### Clause 196 — Enforcement

Material violations of this document shall be subject to investigation, corrective action and the applicable governance and accountability mechanisms.

### Clause 197 — Constitutional Review

This document shall be reviewed periodically and when material legal, regulatory, technological or organisational changes make revision necessary.

### Clause 198 — Amendment Authority

Amendments shall follow the established constitutional document governance process and require the prescribed approval.

Where a material conflict or ambiguity exists, it shall be escalated through the constitutional governance process rather than resolved through unilateral interpretation.

### Clause 200 — Foundational Commitment

Veyra shall develop, acquire, operate, distribute and commercialise its systems with respect for applicable law, intellectual property, individual rights, contractual obligations and the legal responsibilities arising from its activities.

# PART XVI — Implementation Requirements and Governance Instruments

### Clause 201 — Implementation Framework

Veyra shall establish subordinate legal and compliance instruments necessary to implement this Constitution.

### Clause 202 — Legal and Regulatory Register

An operational legal and regulatory register shall identify applicable obligations, responsible entities, owners, controls, evidence and review dates.

### Clause 203 — Intellectual Property Management Standard

Veyra shall establish a standard governing intellectual property creation, acquisition, assignment, licensing, protection, use and enforcement.

### Clause 204 — Software and Open-Source Compliance Standard

Veyra shall establish procedures for software inventory, licence classification, compatibility review, notices, source-disclosure obligations and dependency governance.

### Clause 205 — AI Model and Dataset Rights Standard

Veyra shall establish procedures for recording and assessing model provenance, dataset rights, licences, generated content and third-party AI service terms.

### Clause 206 — Data Protection and Cross-Border Transfer Standard

Veyra shall establish implementation requirements for personal data, lawful processing, data subject rights, international transfers, retention and deletion.

### Clause 207 — Contract Governance Standard

Veyra shall establish procedures governing contract review, approval, authority, execution, monitoring, renewal and termination.

### Clause 208 — Third-Party Legal Due Diligence Standard

Veyra shall establish risk-based procedures for evaluating material suppliers, partners, technology providers and outsourced service relationships.

### Clause 209 — Legal Hold and Evidence Preservation Procedure

Veyra shall establish procedures for identifying, issuing, maintaining, reviewing and releasing legal holds.

### Clause 210 — Regulatory Change Management Procedure

Veyra shall establish procedures for monitoring regulatory developments, assessing impact, assigning action and verifying implementation.

### Clause 211 — Jurisdictional Market Entry Standard

Veyra shall establish a process for legal and regulatory readiness assessments before entering new markets or jurisdictions.

### Clause 212 — Legal Incident and Dispute Procedure

Veyra shall establish procedures for reporting, escalating, investigating, preserving evidence and responding to material legal incidents and disputes.

### Clause 213 — Legal Compliance Training

Personnel and contributors with relevant responsibilities shall receive appropriate training on applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and intellectual property obligations.

### Clause 214 — Implementation Evidence

The existence of the instruments required by this Part shall be supported by approved documents, implementation records and appropriate verification evidence.

### Clause 215 — No Substitution of Constitution

Operational instruments may implement and elaborate this Constitution but shall not replace, contradict or reduce its mandatory requirements.
